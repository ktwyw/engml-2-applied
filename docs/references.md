# Suggested references

The notebooks are self-contained and do not follow any book. The books and articles below are
suggested for further study.

## Books

- T. Hastie, R. Tibshirani and J. Friedman, *The Elements of Statistical Learning*, 2nd ed.
  (Springer, 2009). Splines, principal components, partial least squares, clustering, trees,
  forests and boosting. The authors make the book freely available.
- S. Narasimhan and C. Jordache, *Data Reconciliation and Gross Error Detection: An Intelligent
  Use of Process Data* (Gulf Professional Publishing, 1999).
- S. Boyd and L. Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization* (Cambridge University Press, 2004).
  Equality-constrained least squares and the KKT conditions; freely available from the authors.
- R. E. Barlow, D. J. Bartholomew, J. M. Bremner and H. D. Brunk, *Statistical Inference under
  Order Restrictions* (Wiley, 1972). Isotonic regression.
- P. J. Huber and E. M. Ronchetti, *Robust Statistics*, 2nd ed. (Wiley, 2009).
- H. Martens and T. Næs, *Multivariate Calibration* (Wiley, 1989).
- I. T. Jolliffe, *Principal Component Analysis*, 2nd ed. (Springer, 2002).
- V. Vovk, A. Gammerman and G. Shafer, *Algorithmic Learning in a Random World* (Springer, 2005).
  Conformal prediction.
- H. B. Callen, *Thermodynamics and an Introduction to Thermostatistics*, 2nd ed. (Wiley, 1985).
  Stability, convexity of the thermodynamic potentials and the equal-area construction.

## Articles

**Constraints and reconciliation**

- D. R. Kuehn and H. Davidson, "Computer control. II. Mathematics of control", *Chem. Eng. Prog.*
  **57**(6), 44–47 (1961). The first statement of data reconciliation.
- C. M. Crowe, Y. A. Garcia Campos and A. Hrymak, "Reconciliation of process flow rates by matrix
  projection. Part I: linear case", *AIChE J.* **29**, 881–888 (1983).
- R. S. H. Mah and A. C. Tamhane, "Detection of gross errors in process data", *AIChE J.* **28**,
  828–830 (1982). The measurement test.

**Monotone and robust regression**

- M. Ayer, H. D. Brunk, G. M. Ewing, W. T. Reid and E. Silverman, "An empirical distribution
  function for sampling with incomplete information", *Ann. Math. Stat.* **26**, 641–647 (1955).
  The pool-adjacent-violators algorithm.
- J. O. Ramsay, "Monotone regression splines in action", *Stat. Sci.* **3**, 425–441 (1988).
  M-splines and I-splines.
- P. J. Huber, "Robust estimation of a location parameter", *Ann. Math. Stat.* **35**, 73–101 (1964).
- R. Koenker and G. Bassett, "Regression quantiles", *Econometrica* **46**, 33–50 (1978).

**Chemometrics and process monitoring**

- R. J. Barnes, M. S. Dhanoa and S. J. Lister, "Standard normal variate transformation and
  de-trending of near-infrared diffuse reflectance spectra", *Appl. Spectrosc.* **43**, 772–777 (1989).
- C. Eckart and G. Young, "The approximation of one matrix by another of lower rank",
  *Psychometrika* **1**, 211–218 (1936).
- P. Geladi and B. R. Kowalski, "Partial least-squares regression: a tutorial", *Anal. Chim. Acta*
  **185**, 1–17 (1986).
- S. Wold, M. Sjöström and L. Eriksson, "PLS-regression: a basic tool of chemometrics",
  *Chemom. Intell. Lab. Syst.* **58**, 109–130 (2001).
- J. V. Kresta, J. F. MacGregor and T. E. Marlin, "Multivariate statistical monitoring of process
  operating performance", *Can. J. Chem. Eng.* **69**, 35–47 (1991).

**Clustering**

- S. P. Lloyd, "Least squares quantization in PCM", *IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory* **28**, 129–137 (1982).
- D. Arthur and S. Vassilvitskii, "k-means++: the advantages of careful seeding", in *Proc. 18th
  ACM-SIAM Symposium on Discrete Algorithms* (2007), pp. 1027–1035.
- P. J. Rousseeuw, "Silhouettes: a graphical aid to the interpretation and validation of cluster
  analysis", *J. Comput. Appl. Math.* **20**, 53–65 (1987).
- A. Y. Ng, M. I. Jordan and Y. Weiss, "On spectral clustering: analysis and an algorithm", in
  *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 14* (2001), pp. 849–856.
- U. von Luxburg, "A tutorial on spectral clustering", *Stat. Comput.* **17**, 395–416 (2007).
- A. P. Dempster, N. M. Laird and D. B. Rubin, "Maximum likelihood from incomplete data via the EM
  algorithm", *J. R. Stat. Soc. B* **39**, 1–38 (1977).
- G. Schwarz, "Estimating the dimension of a model", *Ann. Stat.* **6**, 461–464 (1978).

**Trees and ensembles**

- L. Breiman, "Bagging predictors", *Mach. Learn.* **24**, 123–140 (1996).
- L. Breiman, "Random forests", *Mach. Learn.* **45**, 5–32 (2001).
- J. H. Friedman, "Greedy function approximation: a gradient boosting machine", *Ann. Stat.*
  **29**, 1189–1232 (2001).

**Prediction intervals**

- J. Lei, M. G'Sell, A. Rinaldo, R. J. Tibshirani and L. Wasserman, "Distribution-free predictive
  inference for regression", *J. Am. Stat. Assoc.* **113**, 1094–1111 (2018).
- A. N. Angelopoulos and S. Bates, "Conformal prediction: a gentle introduction",
  *Found. Trends Mach. Learn.* **16**, 494–591 (2023).
- P. C. Mahalanobis, "On the generalised distance in statistics", *Proc. Natl. Inst. Sci. India*
  **2**, 49–55 (1936).
